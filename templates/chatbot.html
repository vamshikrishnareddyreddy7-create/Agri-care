AI disclaimer
AI-generated guidance is for assistance. Always follow the equipment manufacturer's service instructions and consult a qualified technician for serious problems.

How the assistant works

Your question is matched against a maintenance knowledge base. Relevant context (including this machine's latest prediction when one is selected) is retrieved, and the answer is generated — by the knowledge base when no LLM key is configured, or by an LLM with that context (RAG) when LLM_API_KEY is set.

The assistant can also explain AI predictions: pick a machine and ask "Why is this machine showing high maintenance risk?"
